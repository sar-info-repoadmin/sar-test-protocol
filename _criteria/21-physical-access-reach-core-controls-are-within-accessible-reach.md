title: Core controls are within accessible reach
category: Physical access / reach
order: 21
expected_behavior: Controls needed for core operation, such as power, cycle selection, settings, start/pause, cancel, and lock controls, can be reached from expected standing and seated positions in the intended installation configuration.
pass_fail_criteria: Pass if users can reach and operate core controls without unsafe posture, excessive stretching, obstruction, or repositioning the appliance. Fail if core controls are too high, too low, too deep, blocked, or only reachable with unsafe or excessive effort.
reference: EN 301 549 5, 8
